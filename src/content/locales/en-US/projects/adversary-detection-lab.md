## What’s in the lab

This cloud SOC lab brings together Caldera, Splunk MLTK, Elastic ML, and Sigma for adversary emulation and detection work.

## Lab use cases

- Generate activity with Caldera and see what appears in the logs.
- Test detection ideas with Sigma, Splunk MLTK, and Elastic ML.
- Look at what context an analyst would need to investigate an alert.

The [GitHub repository](https://github.com/glaciergh0st/AI-APT-detection-lab) has the current setup. This is a personal lab, separate from consulting engagements.
