## The problem

Too much security data can drive up SIEM costs and make investigations harder. Filtering helps only if it keeps the fields and events analysts need.

## The approach

In a consulting engagement, Cribl filtered and normalized security events before they reached the SIEM. Ingestion dropped by 35%.

Validation confirmed that the changes retained fields and event types needed for detections and investigations while removing duplicate and low-value data.

## How the result was evaluated

- Compare ingestion over equivalent time periods.
- Check that detections still have the fields they rely on.
- Review sample alerts and investigations after filtering.
- Keep a rollback plan for parser and routing changes.

The client name and environment details are confidential. The 35% reduction is the result reported during the engagement.
